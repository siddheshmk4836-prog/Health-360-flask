CHAPTER ONE

Understand the disease mix

Compare the number of records associated with each condition. A higher count describes recorded volume; it does not automatically indicate higher prevalence or severity.

Question to explore
Which conditions account for the largest share of records, and does that change across regions?
02
CHAPTER TWO

Look beyond the total

Break records down by risk level. Confirm how risk categories were defined before drawing conclusions or comparing groups.

Question to explore
Which conditions show a larger high-risk share, and what variables may help explain the pattern?
◉
03
CHAPTER THREE

Compare population segments

Age and region comparisons can show where further investigation may help. Interpret counts alongside population size, completeness, and collection methods.

Question to explore
Do patterns persist after accounting for differences in group size?
